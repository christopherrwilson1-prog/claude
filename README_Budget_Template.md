# Student Organization Budget Template
## Complete Financial Planning & Management Toolkit

---

## 📋 Overview

This comprehensive Excel workbook is designed specifically for college student organization treasurers to plan, track, and report on their organization's finances. Whether managing a $2,000 budget or $50,000+, this template provides all the tools needed for professional financial management.

**Target Users:** College student organization treasurers (ages 18-22, varying financial literacy levels)

**Use Cases:**
- Annual budget planning
- Student government allocation requests
- Monthly expense tracking
- Financial reporting to advisors and boards
- Event budget planning

### 3. **Annual Budget** (Your Master Budget)
The foundation of your financial planning.

**Income Section:**
- Student Government Allocation
- Fundraising Revenue
- Membership Dues
- Event Ticket Sales
- Sponsorships
- Donations
- Grants
- Rollover from Previous Year
- Other Income

**Expense Section:**
- Events & Programming
- Food & Catering
- Marketing & Printing
- Travel & Transportation
- Supplies & Materials
- Speaker/Performer Fees
- Equipment & Technology
- Venue Rental
- Merchandise & Apparel
- Administrative Costs
- Professional Development
- Other Expenses
- **Contingency Fund (10% - Auto-calculated)**

**Summary Section:**
- Total Income (auto-calculated)
- Total Expenses (auto-calculated)
- Net Position - shows surplus or deficit
- Budget Status indicator

**How to Use:**
1. Start at the top with organization name and academic year
2. Fill in all expected income sources (be realistic!)
3. Estimate expenses for each category
4. Contingency fund auto-calculates at 10%
5. Check that Net Position is positive (balanced budget)
6. Save and revisit quarterly to adjust projections

**White Cells** = Enter your data here
**Gray Cells** = Auto-calculated (don't edit)

**Pro Tip:** Underestimate income and overestimate expenses to build in a buffer.

### 4. **Semester Breakdown**
Allocate your annual budget between Fall and Spring semesters.

**Features:**
- Split income sources by semester
- Allocate expenses by semester
- Variance column flags misallocations
- Net position by semester

**How to Use:**
1. Annual Total column pulls from Annual Budget sheet
2. Enter Fall Semester amounts for each category
3. Enter Spring Semester amounts
4. Variance column will show $0 if Fall + Spring = Annual Total
5. Non-zero variance? You need to adjust your allocation

**Why This Matters:**
Many orgs spend 60-70% in Fall and 30-40% in Spring due to event timing. This helps you plan cash flow and avoid running out of money mid-year.

**Pro Tip:** Consider when your major events happen and allocate accordingly.

Detailed budget planner for individual events.

**Event Details Section:**
- Event name, date, expected attendance

**Income Section:**

- Other income

**Expense Section:**
- Venue rental
- Food & catering
- Entertainment/speaker fees
- Decorations
- Marketing & promotion
- Supplies & materials
- Equipment rental
- Staff/security

- Other expenses

**Summary Metrics:**
- Total income vs. total expenses
- Net profit/loss

**How to Use:**

### 6. **Monthly Tracker**
Track budgeted vs. actual throughout the year.

**Features:**
- 10 months (August - May academic year)
- Budgeted income per month
- Actual income per month
- Budgeted expenses per month
- Actual expenses per month
- Net position per month (auto-calculated)
- Variance analysis (auto-calculated)
- Year-to-date totals
- Running balance calculator

**Color Coding:**
- **Red cells** = Over budget (need to address)
- **Green cells** = Under budget (doing well)
- **White cells** = No variance

**How to Use:**
1. At the start of each month, enter budgeted amounts
2. Throughout/end of month, enter actual amounts as you spend
3. Variance column shows if you're over or under budget
4. Monitor running balance to ensure you don't overspend
5. Update YTD totals automatically calculate

**Pro Tip:** Update this at least monthly. Weekly updates are even better for staying on track.

**When to Worry:**
- Running balance approaching $0
- Consistent red variance cells
- Actual expenses significantly higher than budgeted

## 💡 Pro Tips & Best Practices

### Budgeting Tips

**Income Projections:**
- ✅ Be conservative - better to underestimate
- ✅ Don't count on uncertain income (maybes)
- ✅ Base on previous year performance
- ❌ Don't assume 100% fundraising success

**Expense Planning:**
- ✅ Add 10-15% buffer to estimates
- ✅ Get real quotes, don't guess
- ✅ Always include contingency fund
- ✅ Plan for price increases
- ❌ Don't forget "hidden costs" (tax, delivery, setup)

**General:**
- ✅ Update regularly (monthly minimum)
- ✅ Keep receipts for everything
- ✅ Communicate with exec board
- ✅ Review budget quarterly
- ✅ Learn from variances
- ❌ Don't make big purchases without checking budget
- ❌ Don't commit to expenses you can't cover

### Allocation Request Tips

**Do:**
- ✅ Know every line item cold
- ✅ Bring quotes and documentation
- ✅ Show how money benefits students
- ✅ Demonstrate past responsible spending
- ✅ Be honest about challenges
- ✅ Practice your presentation
- ✅ Dress professionally
- ✅ Arrive early
- ✅ Thank the board

**Don't:**
- ❌ Inflate numbers
- ❌ Request more than you can spend
- ❌ Skip justifications
- ❌ Hide past overspending
- ❌ Be defensive when questioned
- ❌ Make promises you can't keep

### Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Not updating regularly** - Budget is useless if not maintained
2. **Forgetting contingency** - Stuff happens, plan for it
3. **Overestimating income** - Creates phantom money you don't have
4. **Underestimating food costs** - Food always costs more than you think
5. **Ignoring past data** - History is your best predictor
6. **Making commitments before checking budget** - Check first, commit second
7. **Not communicating** - Tell your exec board about financial status
8. **Waiting until crisis** - Small issues become big problems
9. **Losing receipts** - Document everything
10. **Not planning for seasonality** - Some semesters cost more

## 🎓 Understanding Key Concepts

### Budget Status Indicators

**Balanced Budget:**
- Total Income = Total Expenses
- Net Position = $0 or positive
- ✅ Good - shows responsible planning

**Surplus:**
- Total Income > Total Expenses
- Net Position = Positive number
- ✅ Good - but make sure you're not under-serving members

**Deficit:**
- Total Income < Total Expenses
- Net Position = Negative number
- ⚠️ Problem - you're planning to spend money you don't have

### Variance Analysis

**Positive Variance:**
- Income: Earned more than budgeted (good!)
- Expenses: Spent more than budgeted (bad!)

**Negative Variance:**
- Income: Earned less than budgeted (bad!)
- Expenses: Spent less than budgeted (good!)

**Color Coding in Monthly Tracker:**
- **Green** = Favorable variance
- **Red** = Unfavorable variance
- **White** = On target

### Cash Flow

**Why It Matters:**
You might have a balanced annual budget but still run out of money if timing is off.

**Example:**
- Annual budget: $10,000 income, $10,000 expenses (balanced)
- But if you spend $7,000 in September and don't get funding until October...
- You have a cash flow problem!

**Solution:**
Use Semester Breakdown and Monthly Tracker to plan when money comes in vs. goes out.

## 📊 Troubleshooting

### Common Issues & Solutions

**Problem:** Formulas showing #REF! error
- **Cause:** Rows/columns were deleted
- **Fix:** Undo deletion or manually fix formula references

**Problem:** Totals not calculating
- **Cause:** Input cells contain text instead of numbers
- **Fix:** Remove any non-numeric characters ($, commas, text)

**Problem:** Budget not balancing
- **Cause:** Income < Expenses
- **Fix:** Increase income projections or decrease expenses

**Problem:** Variance column not showing $0 on Semester Breakdown
- **Cause:** Fall + Spring doesn't equal Annual Total
- **Fix:** Adjust Fall or Spring amounts to match

**Problem:** Conditional formatting not working
- **Cause:** Excel version compatibility
- **Fix:** Recreate conditional formatting rules

**Problem:** Print layout looks wrong
- **Cause:** Default page setup
- **Fix:** Page Layout → Adjust margins, orientation, scaling

## 📚 Glossary

**Allocation:** Funding provided by student government from student activity fees

**Balanced Budget:** When total projected income equals total projected expenses

**Cash Flow:** The timing of when money comes in versus goes out

**Contingency Fund:** Money set aside for unexpected expenses (typically 10% of budget)

**Deficit:** When expenses exceed income (negative net position)

**Expense:** Money going out of your organization

**Fixed Costs:** Expenses that stay the same regardless of activity (subscriptions, insurance)

**Income:** Money coming into your organization

**Line Item:** A single entry in the budget for a specific category

**Net Position:** The difference between total income and total expenses (surplus or deficit)

**Rollover:** Funds carried over from the previous fiscal year

**Surplus:** When income exceeds expenses (positive net position)

**Variable Costs:** Expenses that change based on activity level (event food, printing per event)

**Variance:** The difference between budgeted and actual amounts

**YTD (Year-to-Date):** Cumulative total from start of fiscal year to current date
