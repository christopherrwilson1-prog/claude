# Business Model & Market Analysis

## Executive Summary

**Student Org Budget** is a mobile-first SaaS application designed specifically for college student organization treasurers to manage budgets, track expenses, and generate professional financial reports. The app addresses a clear pain point in the $500M+ student organization financial management market with a focused, affordable solution.

**Revenue Model:** $5/month per organization subscription
**Target Market:** 50,000+ student organizations across 2,000+ US colleges
**Initial Goal:** 100 paying organizations ($500 MRR) within 6 months

---

## Market Opportunity

### Market Size

**Total Addressable Market (TAM):**
- 4,000+ degree-granting institutions in the US
- Average 150-300 student organizations per campus
- Estimated 600,000+ student organizations nationwide
- **TAM: $36M annual revenue** at $5/month/org

**Serviceable Available Market (SAM):**
- Focus on 4-year universities (2,000+)
- Organizations with budgets $2K-$50K
- Tech-savvy student leaders
- **SAM: $12M annual revenue** (~200,000 orgs)

**Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM):**
- Initial focus: Medium-large universities (500)
- Target 5% penetration in year 1
- **SOM Year 1: $300K annual revenue** (~5,000 orgs)

### Market Characteristics

**Student Organization Types:**
1. **Academic Clubs** (30%) - Subject-specific, professional development
2. **Cultural Organizations** (20%) - Heritage, international student groups
3. **Greek Life** (15%) - Fraternities, sororities
4. **Service Organizations** (15%) - Community service, volunteer
5. **Social/Special Interest** (20%) - Hobbies, interests

**Budget Ranges:**
- Small orgs: $500-$2,000/year
- Medium orgs: $2,000-$10,000/year
- Large orgs: $10,000-$50,000/year
- Greek/Major orgs: $50,000-$500,000/year

**Pain Points:**
- Manual spreadsheet management (80% use Excel/Google Sheets)
- Difficulty tracking expenses across semester
- Unprofessional budget presentations
- Lack of collaboration tools
- Annual treasurer turnover (knowledge loss)
- Student government allocation requirements

## Competitive Analysis

### Direct Competitors

**1. QuickBooks Online**
- **Price:** $15-30/month
- **Pros:** Full-featured, recognized brand
- **Cons:** Too complex, not student-focused, expensive
- **Our Advantage:** 3-6x cheaper, purpose-built for student orgs

**2. Mint (Intuit)**
- **Price:** Free
- **Pros:** Personal finance tracking
- **Cons:** Not designed for organizations, no collaboration
- **Our Advantage:** Multi-user, org-specific features, professional reports

**3. Wave Accounting**
- **Price:** Free (invoicing fees)
- **Pros:** Free, small business focused
- **Cons:** Too complex, not student-focused, desktop-first
- **Our Advantage:** Mobile-first, student UX, simpler

### Indirect Competitors

**4. Google Sheets/Excel**
- **Price:** Free
- **Pros:** Familiar, flexible
- **Cons:** Manual, no structure, no automation, no mobile
- **Our Advantage:** Automated calculations, mobile, professional templates

**5. University-Provided Tools**
- **Price:** Free (to students)
- **Pros:** Integrated with university systems
- **Cons:** Often outdated, not user-friendly, limited features
- **Our Advantage:** Modern UX, always accessible, better features

## Revenue Model

### Pricing Strategy

**Primary Plan: Organization Subscription**
- **$5/month per organization**
- Billed monthly (Apple In-App Purchase)
- Unlimited users per organization
- All features included
- 7-day free trial

**Pricing Psychology:**
- Under typical "coffee test" ($5 = 1 latte)
- Perceived as "cheap" compared to $15-30 alternatives
- Annual org budgets are $2K-$50K - $60/year is negligible
- Student government often reimburses tools

### Revenue Projections

**Conservative Scenario:**
| Month | Orgs | MRR | ARR |
|-------|------|-----|-----|
| 1 | 10 | $50 | $600 |
| 3 | 30 | $150 | $1,800 |
| 6 | 100 | $500 | $6,000 |
| 12 | 250 | $1,250 | $15,000 |
| 24 | 750 | $3,750 | $45,000 |

**Optimistic Scenario:**
| Month | Orgs | MRR | ARR |
|-------|------|-----|-----|
| 1 | 20 | $100 | $1,200 |
| 3 | 75 | $375 | $4,500 |
| 6 | 250 | $1,250 | $15,000 |
| 12 | 750 | $3,750 | $45,000 |
| 24 | 2,500 | $12,500 | $150,000 |

**Key Assumptions:**
- 7-day trial → 20-30% conversion
- Monthly churn: 5-8% (academic year seasonality)
- Viral coefficient: 0.3-0.5 (orgs refer other orgs)
- Customer acquisition cost: $10-15 (organic + university partnerships)

### Unit Economics

**Per Organization:**
- Revenue: $5/month = $60/year
- Apple Fee (30%): -$1.50/month = -$18/year
- **Net Revenue: $3.50/month = $42/year**

**Costs Per Organization:**
- Supabase (pro tier): ~$0.05/month
- Storage: ~$0.02/month
- Support: ~$0.10/month (amortized)
- **Total Cost: ~$0.17/month**

**Gross Margin: 95%**
**LTV (24-month average tenure): $84**
**CAC Target: <$20**
**LTV:CAC Ratio: >4:1** ✅

## Retention & Growth

### Retention Strategy

**Product Stickiness:**
- Data lock-in (budget history)
- Team collaboration (multi-user)
- Academic year cycles (natural retention)
- Professional reports (ongoing need)

**Churn Reduction:**
- Onboarding excellence (reduce early churn)
- Regular feature updates (maintain interest)
- Proactive support (prevent frustration)
- Annual pricing (lock in longer)

**Expected Churn:**
- Month 1-3: 15-20% (not a good fit)
- Month 4-12: 5-8% (normal churn)
- Month 12+: 3-5% (mature customers)
- **Annual churn: ~40%** (academic year turnover)

### Growth Loops

**Loop 1: Word of Mouth**
```
New org signs up
    ↓
Creates professional budget
    ↓
Presents to student government
    ↓
```

**Loop 2: University Partnerships**

```
    ↓

    ↓
```

**Loop 3: Viral Coefficient**

## Risk Analysis

### Business Risks

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|--------|-----------|
| Low trial conversion | High | Improve onboarding, extend trial |
| High churn | High | Better product, proactive support |
| Competitor emerges | Medium | Focus on student niche, build moat |
| Seasonality (summer) | Medium | Annual pricing, enterprise deals |
| University budget cuts | Low | Still cheaper than alternatives |

### Technical Risks

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|--------|-----------|
| Supabase downtime | Medium | Caching, status page, SLA |
| Data loss | High | Daily backups, redundancy |
| Security breach | High | Audit, encryption, insurance |
| Scaling issues | Medium | Monitor, optimize early |

### Market Risks

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|--------|-----------|
| Low market need | High | Validate with beta users first |
| Price resistance | Medium | Flexible pricing, free tier |
| COVID impact | Low | Virtual events still need budgets |
